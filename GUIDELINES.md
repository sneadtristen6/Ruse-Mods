# Guidelines for the mod list

Every mod on this list shows up in every player's RUSE Launcher, so each one is checked before it's added. These are
the rules. They're here to keep players' PCs, games and Steam accounts safe, and to keep the list honest.

## Welcome

Units and balance, maps, Operations and missions, game modes, AI, sound, looks and text: made with RUSE Studio, with
RUSE Mod Manager, or both.

## Not on the list

1. **Programs, or anything that runs on your PC.** No `.exe`, `.dll`, installers or scripts for Windows. RUSE Launcher
   refuses them anyway.
2. **Changes to the game's install or its program.** A mod changes the game's data in a separate modded copy, and the
   Steam game stays as it is. Nothing that patches R.U.S.E. itself.
3. **Unexplained changes to the game's own scripts.** Some mods change one of the game's scripts (Formations, missions,
   game modes). That's allowed when the form says which scripts and what each change does, and we've read it before
   listing the mod. Its entry says it changes a script.
4. **Anything that touches Steam accounts,** game ownership, achievements or leaderboards, or that tries to get around
   them.
5. **Cheating against other players.** Cheats and test tools are fine for playing offline and testing. They go in
   their own group, tagged as cheats, and are never installed unless a player picks them.
6. **Other people's work without permission.** Name everyone whose models, pictures, sounds, music, maps or code are in
   your mod, and only include what their licence or their OK allows.
7. **Hateful content,** or anything meant to harass people, or that breaks the law.
8. **A description that doesn't match the file.** What the form says the mod does, changes and runs has to be complete
   and true.

## How a mod gets on the list

1. Open **[Add my mod](https://github.com/sneadtristen6/Ruse-Mods/issues/new?template=add-mod.yml)** (RUSE Studio's
   **Publish** opens it filled in) and attach your mod as a `.zip`.
2. An automatic check reads the file (it never runs anything in it) and comments within a few minutes. It refuses
   programs and anything that writes outside the game's data, and marks mods that change the game's scripts for a
   closer look.
3. One of us reads the form, tries the mod, and adds it to the list, or says here what needs changing.
4. An update goes the same way: send the new version with what changed.

We can take a mod off the list if it turns out to break these rules. If you think we got it wrong, say so on the
issue.

## Credit

Mods made with RUSE Studio carry a "Made with RUSE Studio" line, and the list shows it. Please keep it when you share
your mod elsewhere: it helps other players find the tools.
